📖 What is this? — Explain like I'm 5 ▼
😩
The Problem
Paying on Ethereum normally costs a gas fee every single transaction. If you bought 4 drinks at a bar, that's 4 separate fees — slow, expensive, and annoying.

Credit cards charge the venue 2.5–3% per transaction, and customers can chargeback after the fact — leaving the bar unpaid.
💡
The Big Idea — State Channels
Imagine you open a tab at a bar. Instead of paying per drink, you leave your card behind the bar as a deposit. Every drink just updates a running total — no fee, instant.

At the end of the night, only one final payment actually happens. That's exactly what InstantTab does, but on Ethereum — trustlessly.
⚙️
How It Works — Step by Step
ON-CHAIN
1. Lock collateral. Customer sends ETH to a smart contract — like leaving cash with the bartender. No one can take it without your signature.
OFF-CHAIN
2. Order drinks freely. Every drink creates a tiny signed message (EIP-712) — like a digitally signed IOU. Both customer and merchant sign it. Zero gas, instant, stored in the backend database.
ON-CHAIN
3. Close the tab. Merchant submits the final signed message to the blockchain. The contract checks both signatures are valid and the nonce is correct.
GUARD
4. Challenge window (10 min). A backend watcher monitors for fraud — e.g. if the merchant tries to submit an old state with a smaller total. If caught, the merchant gets 100% slashed.
ON-CHAIN
5. Settle. After the window, anyone calls withdraw(). Merchant gets what they earned. Customer gets unused collateral back. Protocol takes a tiny fee.
🔑
Key Concepts
EIP-712 A standard way to sign structured data on Ethereum. Makes signatures human-readable and tamper-proof.
Nonce A counter that only goes up. Prevents anyone from replaying an old message as if it were the latest.
State Channel Two parties exchange signed messages off-chain, and only settle the final result on-chain. Like keeping score on paper and only paying at the end of the game.
Challenge Guard A robot (backend watcher) that watches the blockchain. If the merchant cheats by submitting an old score, the watcher submits proof and the cheater loses everything.
UUPS Proxy The smart contract can be upgraded safely. A 48-hour timelock means no surprise changes — users always have time to exit first.
🏆
The Result
✔ 0 gas fees per drink — only 2 on-chain transactions total per session
✔ Zero walkouts — collateral is pre-locked, merchant always gets paid
✔ No chargebacks — both parties signed every state
✔ Self-custody — the smart contract holds the funds, not a company
✔ Fraud-proof — challenge window punishes any attempt to cheat
